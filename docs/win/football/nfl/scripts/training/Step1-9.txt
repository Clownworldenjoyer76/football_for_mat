NFL Historical Training Pipeline — Steps 1–9

1. Step 1 — Build historical core
    * Builds the combined 2021–2025 historical training table from historical game data.
2. Step 2 — Add odds/weather
    * Adds historical betting odds and base weather/game-environment fields.
3. Step 3 — Add final-game targets
    * Creates final outcome fields such as win/loss, ATS result, totals result, margins, and scoring targets.
4. Step 4 — Apply historical enrichment rules
    * Adds ML, ATS, and totals historical rule matches, counts, IDs, and strongest-rule metadata.
5. Step 5 — Add lagged team stats
    * Adds Week N-1 team statistics for 12 team metrics.
    * Week 1 is initially blank.
    step5_split.py
    * Splits the combined table into:
        * historical_core_2021.csv
        * historical_core_2022.csv
        * historical_core_2023.csv
        * historical_core_2024.csv
        * historical_core_2025.csv
6. Step 6 — Add lagged QB stats
    * Adds each starting QB’s latest prior-week same-season statistics.
    * Uses 6 QB metrics.
    * Week 1 is initially blank.
7. Step 7 — Fill Week 1 team/QB values
    * Uses the final available prior-season team and QB rows for Week 1.
    * 2022 uses 2021 data, 2023 uses 2022, etc.
    * 2021 remains unchanged because 2020 team/QB source files do not exist.
8. Step 8 — Rolled into Step 9
    * Existing rest/schedule/venue/weather fields were already present.
    * Only rest_diff = home_rest - away_rest still needed to be added.
9. Step 9 — Add rest difference + travel
    * Adds:
        * rest_diff
        * miles_traveled
        * time_zones_crossed
        * east_to_west
        * west_to_east
        * international_flag
        * neutral_site_flag
    * Uses historical games plus team_master.csv coordinates/timezones.
    * Includes historical team abbreviation normalization such as WAS -> WSH.
    * 2025 international-game flags were specifically corrected.

Current outputs: the five annual historical_core_2021.csv through historical_core_2025.csv files are the working training tables updated in place through Step 9.
