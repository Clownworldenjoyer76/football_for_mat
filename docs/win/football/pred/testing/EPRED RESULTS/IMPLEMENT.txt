The clearest actionable EPRED items, **ignoring projected score and total**, are:

* **Use `rating_gap = selected_team_rating - opponent_rating` as a major reliability input.**

  * Rating gap < 0: **42.33%**
  * 0–2.9: **51.19%**
  * 3–5.9: **66.75%**
  * 6–9.9: **75.25%**
  * 10+: **79.90%**

* **Use EPRED probability as a separate confidence input.**

  * 50–54.9%: **53.19%**
  * 55–59.9%: **56.34%**
  * 60–64.9%: **62.78%**
  * 65–69.9%: **68.63%**
  * 75–79.9%: **75.00%**
  * 80–84.9%: **83.12%**

* **Reduce EPRED reliability early in the season.** Weeks 1–3 were weaker than later weeks.

* **Account for home/away.** EPRED selections were **65.36% at home vs 60.73% away**.

* **Treat `matchupQuality` as context, not team strength.** High matchup quality did not mean the selected team was more likely to win.

So the useful pipeline addition is an **EPRED reliability component** based mainly on:

`EPRED probability + rating gap + home/away + week`

The biggest finding is the **rating gap**. If EPRED picks a team but its own rating has that team below its opponent, that was historically a bad sign.
